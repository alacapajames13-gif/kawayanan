Slower mornings, cooler afternoons

Tucked where the bamboo groves meet the riverside, Kawayanan KaiMig has welcomed families and barkada trips for weekend swims and long lunches under the cottages. Every unit is named after the bamboo and nipa materials it's built from — a small nod to how we build, and how we like to keep things simple.

Bamboo Cottages

Six shaded cottages, from cozy nooks for two to family-sized houses that sleep ten.

Three Pools

A kiddie pool, an adult lap pool, and the KaiMig Lagoon — our biggest and coolest.

Riverside Dining

Bring your own ihaw-ihaw or order from our small kitchen — tables open by the water.

Events & Occasions

Book the whole grove for reunions, birthdays, and team outings — ask our staff.
